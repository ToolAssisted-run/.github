# toolAssisted.run — Discord Server Rules

These rules exist so everyone can enjoy the hobby. They follow the same
principles as [our Community Principles](https://github.com/ToolAssisted-run#1-community-principles): the runs
are the agenda, good faith is the default, and credit is sacred.

**1. Stay on game.**
This server is about TAS, speedrunning, and gaming. Take everything else —
especially politics, religion, and other controversial topics — somewhere
else. Gaming is fun; politics is not.

**2. Be excellent to each other.**
No harassment, hate, bullying, slurs, or personal attacks. Disagree with an
idea as hard as you like; leave the person alone.

**3. Assume good faith.**
Give people the benefit of the doubt. Newcomers' questions are welcome, nobody
gets mocked for not knowing something, and past mistakes don't follow people
around forever.

**4. Never claim work that isn't yours.**
Credit authors — human and tool — when you share or build on a run. Disclosing
your methods is a badge, never a confession. Plagiarism is the one offense
this community treats as unforgivable.

**5. Every tool is welcome.**
Savestates, Lua, bots, solvers, machine learning — we document methods, we
don't ban them. Don't dump on how someone else makes their runs.

**6. Keep it legal.**
Don't share or request copyrighted ROMs, ISOs, or BIOS files, and no piracy,
scams, or malware links. Don't put the project's legal standing at risk.

**7. No spam, no drive-by promotion.**
Sharing your own TAS work and tools is encouraged — in the channels meant for
it. Repeated self-promo, mass pings, and off-server advertising get removed.

**8. Keep it safe for work.**
Content should be fine for anyone to open on any screen.

**9. Moderation happens in the open.**
Use channels for their stated topic and follow moderator requests. Every
moderation action is logged together with the rule it enforces; if you think a
call was wrong, appeal it openly. Nobody is ever sanctioned for what they
believe — only for what they do to other participants here.

**10. Discord's Terms of Service apply everywhere.**

*TL;DR: talk about games, be kind, credit people, and enjoy pushing games
beyond human limits.*
