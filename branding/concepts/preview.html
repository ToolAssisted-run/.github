ToolAssisted.run — Logo Concepts

Four directions: modern geometric marks and typography-led wordmarks. Ink adapts to background; the accent is a constant "verified green" — the color of a passing sync check.

Concept 1 — Frame Advance

The single most iconic TAS gesture: play one frame, exactly. Play triangle in accent green plus the advance bar. Icon works standalone as favicon/avatar.

Concept 2 — Play-Dot

Pure wordmark: the "." of the domain hack becomes a play button. The name is the logo; nothing else needed. All-monospace for engineering precision.

Concept 3 — Input Timeline

A TAStudio-style piano roll: pressed inputs behind a green playhead, future frames ahead of it. The most literal "this is how TAS is made" mark; richest at larger sizes.

Concept 4 — Executable

The domain hack taken literally: the site is a script you execute. Speaks directly to the automated-pipeline identity. The cursor doubles as a live/active signal.
